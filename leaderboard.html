How Points Are Calculated

Points are earned per match based on final placement and total eliminations.

Placement Points
1st → 10 pts · 2nd → 6 pts · 3rd → 5 pts · 4th → 4 pts · 5th → 3 pts
Kill Points
+1 point per elimination, added to your placement points.
Total Score
Total Points = Placement + Kills across all matches played.
Tiebreaker
In case of equal points, the team with more wins ranks higher.
