01

General Rules

By registering for any BCE Esports tournament, all team captains and their registered players agree to be bound by these rules. These rules apply to every tournament unless a specific tournament page states additional or overriding rules.

BCE Esports reserves the right to modify these rules at any time. Registered teams will be notified of any material changes via email or the tournament page, at least 48 hours before the affected matches begin.

Each tournament has a fixed registration window. Registrations submitted after the closing date/time will not be accepted, even if slots remain. Times are always in Indian Standard Time (IST).

If more registrations are received than available slots, teams will be added to a waitlist on a first-come, first-served basis. Waitlisted teams may be promoted if confirmed teams withdraw.

All official tournament communication will be sent to the captain's registered email. It is the captain's responsibility to:

  • Monitor the registered email regularly (including spam folder).
  • Join the official BCE Esports Discord server for real-time updates.
  • Relay important information to their teammates.
⚠️
Missing a match because you didn't check your email or Discord is not grounds for appeal. It is the captain's responsibility to stay updated.
02

Eligibility

Eligibility depends on the tournament's category:

  • Intra-College: All players must be currently enrolled students at Bakhtiyarpur College of Engineering (BCE) with a valid ID card.
  • Inter-College: Players must be enrolled at a recognised college listed in the tournament's eligible institutions list.
  • Open: Any player can participate, subject to game-specific age ratings and rules.
ℹ️
If you have recently graduated but are within 6 months of your last exam, you may still be eligible. Contact esports@bcebakhtiyarpur.ac.in for individual verification.

For Intra-College tournaments, the captain must register using a valid @bcebakhtiyarpur.ac.in email address. This is to prevent external players from joining college-only events.

Inter-college and open tournaments do not require this verification, though additional documentation may be requested for prize distribution.

  • Players must be at least 16 years old at the time of registration.
  • Players must own their in-game accounts. Account sharing, purchase, or transfer is prohibited.
  • In-game account names must be appropriate and non-offensive. Offensive names will result in disqualification.
03

Team Composition

Each tournament page specifies the required team size. You must submit exactly the required number of main players. For example, in BGMI squad tournaments this is 4 main players.

Some tournaments allow 1 optional substitute. Substitutes must meet the same eligibility criteria as main players.

⚠️
Registrations with incorrect roster sizes (too many or too few) will be rejected during verification.

A player may register for multiple tournaments across different games, but within a single tournament, a player may only be on one team.

Any player appearing on two rosters in the same tournament will have all their registrations flagged for review, and both teams may face disqualification.

Roster changes may be requested via email before the registration closing date. After that date, roster changes are not permitted except in exceptional cases (medical emergencies, family emergencies) approved by the organisers.

Substitutes may replace main players between matches but not mid-match, unless approved by a referee.

04

Match Rules

Teams must join the match lobby at least 10 minutes before the scheduled start time. Late teams will face penalties:

  • 5+ minutes late: Warning issued.
  • 10+ minutes late: Walkover (W.O.) awarded to the opposing team.
  • 15+ minutes late: Immediate disqualification from the tournament.

Map pools, server regions, and pick/ban procedures are defined per tournament on the tournament page. For BGMI/Free Fire/CODM, standard tournament maps are used. For Valorant, the map veto is conducted in the lobby before the match.

Servers are hosted in the India/South Asia region. If a player has persistent lag issues, they should report this to a referee before the match begins.

  • Mobile games (BGMI, FF, CODM): Mobile phones or tablets only. Emulators are strictly prohibited.
  • Valorant: PC only, with stable internet connection (minimum 10 Mbps recommended).
  • Chess: Played on the designated online platform; link shared before the tournament.
⚠️
Using an emulator in a mobile-game tournament is an instant disqualification offence — no exceptions.
05

Code of Conduct

All players, teams, and spectators must treat others with respect. The following are strictly prohibited:

  • Abusive, racist, sexist, or discriminatory language in any game chat, Discord, or social media.
  • Harassment, bullying, or targeted mockery of other players.
  • Threats of violence, doxxing, or sharing another player's personal information.
  • Deliberately provocative behaviour (trolling) intended to upset others.

Penalties range from a formal warning to immediate disqualification, at the organiser's discretion.

Any form of cheating is an immediate disqualification offence. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Use of hacks, aim-bots, wall-hacks, or modified game files.
  • Team identification to gain unfair advantage (teaming in solo modes).
  • Deliberate use of game glitches or exploits.
  • Third-party software or scripts that provide an unfair advantage.
  • Account sharing or playing under a false identity.
⚠️
Cheating results in permanent ban from all BCE Esports tournaments and forfeiture of any prize money won during that tournament.

BCE Esports retains the exclusive right to broadcast and stream all tournament matches. Players and teams:

  • May stream their own POV with a minimum 2-minute delay, unless the tournament is flagged as "no personal streams".
  • May not monetise their personal streams during BCE Esports tournaments without prior written permission.
  • Must not share streams to reveal positions of other teams mid-match.
06

Payment & Refunds

Entry fees are listed on each tournament page. Free tournaments require no payment. For paid tournaments:

  • Pay the exact amount to the UPI ID shown on the registration page.
  • Take a screenshot of the successful transaction showing the UTR number.
  • Upload the screenshot when registering.

Registrations with missing or invalid payment proof will remain pending and may be rejected after the registration window closes.

Refunds are issued in the following cases:

  • Tournament cancelled by organisers: 100% refund.
  • Your registration rejected before tournament start: 100% refund.
  • You withdraw 7+ days before start: 100% refund.
  • You withdraw 3–7 days before start: 50% refund.
  • You withdraw fewer than 3 days before start: No refund.

Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 7–10 business days.

07

Disqualification & Penalties

The following offences result in immediate disqualification, without warning:

  • Any form of cheating (hacks, exploits, teaming).
  • Use of emulators in mobile-game tournaments.
  • Falsifying eligibility documents or identity.
  • Registering multiple times with different emails/phones.
  • Threatening or assaulting another player or organiser.

For lesser offences, penalties escalate progressively:

  1. First offence: Formal warning, recorded on the team's tournament file.
  2. Second offence: Point deduction (typically 5–10 points).
  3. Third offence: Match forfeiture or tournament disqualification.

Serious offences may skip levels at the organiser's discretion.

08

Disputes & Appeals

If you believe a match result, disqualification, or other decision was unfair, you must:

  1. File your dispute within 24 hours of the incident.
  2. Email esports@bcebakhtiyarpur.ac.in with subject "Dispute — [Tournament Name] — [Match ID]".
  3. Include all evidence: screenshots, video clips, timestamps, and a clear description of what happened.

Disputes filed after the 24-hour window will not be considered, unless exceptional circumstances apply.

All disputes are reviewed by the tournament organisers within 48 hours. If the dispute concerns an ongoing match, the match may be paused while evidence is reviewed.

The decision of the organisers is final and binding. Persistent filing of frivolous disputes may result in penalties against the filing team.

ℹ️
For allegations of cheating, evidence must include video footage. Screenshots alone are not sufficient.
